Privacy Policy

Bubble Blast · last updated 10 September 2026
In short: Bubble Blast runs completely offline. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party servers. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. Specifically, the game never asks for or sends:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • your contact list, photos, files or messages;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • device identifiers used for advertising or profiling.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps the following data in its own private storage area (Android SharedPreferences). It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game:

  • the highest level you have unlocked and the stars earned per level;
  • your best score for each level, plus your coin balance and power-up inventory;
  • your sound, music, vibration, aim-guide and handedness preferences;
  • the day of your last daily reward, so the reward cannot be claimed twice.

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

3. Permissions

The game requests a single permission, VIBRATE, and only to give haptic feedback when bubbles pop. It can be switched off in Settings. The game does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone or location access.

4. Advertising and purchases

This build contains no advertising SDKs and no in-app purchases. All shop items are bought with coins earned by playing. If a future release adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that release ships, and any ad provider will be required to follow the same rules described here.

5. Children's privacy

Bubble Blast is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data at all, no data is collected from children either.

6. Security

Your save data is stored inside the app's private sandbox, which other apps cannot read. There is no server, so there is no data at rest outside your device.

7. Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or processed, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Removing the game, or using "Reset progress", removes every trace of your play session from the device.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be bundled with the next update of the game and shown in this screen, with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer address listed on the Bubble Blast store page.
